Encounter overview

One fight, four lessons

Each phase teaches one rule, then later phases combine those rules under greater pressure. Difficulty comes from decisions and positioning—not hidden damage or arbitrary support removal.

Encounter promise

  • Royal Lizard always enters with two distinct random Lizard-family adds.
  • No unrelated guardians: no Goliath, Windra, or equivalent substitutions.
  • SeaWave always approaches from the enemy court and retains native damage and tumble.
  • The encounter scripts never cast Magma or Inferno. The stock client and server leave the retail stage's native Magma behavior untouched.

Player mastery arc

  • Read: identify the active rule from chat and attack language.
  • Respond: move, switch targets, or control the tennis point.
  • Coordinate: spread for Convergence, regroup briefly for recovery.
  • Execute: choose between killing adds and burning the boss.

PHASE 1

The First Tide

Teach add priority, wave reading, and controlled support scarcity.

Opening
Royal Lizard is immune while either attendant lives. The boss remains silent; both adds use SeaWave and Blizzard on 6–12s attack intervals. Healing and shields initially work normally.
Add at 50%
The First Tide turns. Guardian spells stop. Healing and shields are disabled. Three-wave volleys begin: 1s spacing, random X and random enemy-court depth, then a random 4–5s rest.
First add dies
Five-Wave Rage. Volleys grow to five waves with random X and a random safe enemy-court depth per wave. One random player receives Tide’s Grace and alone retains healing and shields.
Both adds die
The ward breaks. Royal Lizard becomes vulnerable. A new random player receives Tide’s Grace for the Crown’s first reckoning.
100–90%
Royal Lizard attacks every 4s with SeaWave, Homing Ball, or Blizzard. The boss is now the clear focus target.
90–70%
Only Homing Ball remains in the boss attack loop, alongside the learned randomized five-wave volleys. Tide’s Grace remains on the announced player.

What players should learn

  • The adds are the boss’s living shield.
  • Three waves become five after the first kill.
  • Random X prevents rote lane camping.
  • The named support player becomes a temporary team role.

Failure pressure

  • Ignoring adds produces zero boss progress.
  • Poor movement compounds repeated volleys.
  • Losing the Tide’s Grace player removes the team’s only support access during suppression.
Transition: at 70% HP, Royal Lizard sinks into the drowned court. The severed bloodlines begin to stir.

PHASE 2

The Twin Tides

Turn target selection into a coordination challenge, then demand a synchronized execution.

Phase setup

  • Both attendants return at 60% HP through separate rebirth casts into their original left and right slots.
  • Royal Lizard is immune while either lives.
  • Both adds remain vulnerable within 10%; at 10%+ imbalance, the lower-health add is protected until players restore balance.
  • Healing works at 20%; shields are enabled.
  • Royal Lizard casts Homing Ball every 10s.

Core player question

Can the team distribute damage evenly, then commit to a clean ten-second double kill?

This phase punishes tunnel vision without asking players to stop attacking.

PHASE 2 · THE TWIN TIDES04
PHASE 3

The Rising Tide

Make the tennis point itself the difficulty controller.

Simple rule

+1

Player team loses a point: Tide rises one level.

−1

Players win a point against Royal Lizard: Tide recedes one level.

What does not change Tide

  • Royal Lizard’s current HP
  • Direct skill damage
  • Elapsed phase time

Phase 3 begins at Maximum Tide. The rule is fully observable: play better tennis to lower encounter pressure.

LevelActive attack setPlayer read
Low Tide · 0Homing Ball every 10sStable baseline; build momentum.
Rising Tide · 1+ Water Pillar every 15sMovement begins competing with point play.
High Tide · 2+ Blizzard every 20sCourt control narrows.
Maximum Tide · 3+ Storm every 30sFull pressure; win the next point.
Cumulative, not replacement: higher levels retain all lower-level attacks. Only one overdue attack fires per update, with at least 2s between boss casts.

Phase boundaries

  • Begins with Royal Lizard at 70% HP.
  • Always starts at Maximum Tide.
  • Ends when Royal Lizard reaches 25% HP.
  • Healing remains at 20%; shields remain enabled.

Anti-snowball intent

The phase opens at full pressure, but one won point immediately begins recovery. The mechanic rewards composure instead of requiring a damage-race solution.

Transition: driven to the brink, Royal Lizard sinks beneath Atlantis. The Drowned Crown awakens.
PHASE 3 · THE RISING TIDE05
PHASE 4

The Drowned Crown

A final-act synthesis: resurrection, healing pressure, target choice, inherited power, and execution.

5s calm
Royal Lizard rises from 25% to 35% HP. Both attendants return at 30% HP through separate rebirth casts into their original left and right slots. Full player healing is briefly available; shields remain enabled.
Blood Tide
Every player-side point loss heals Royal Lizard by 2% maximum HP, capped at 35%, and each living add by 8%. Players must keep winning points while choosing targets.
Boss pressure
Homing Ball 8s · Water Pillar 12s. Each living add attacks every 8–12s with SeaWave or Blizzard. Boss casts maintain a 1.5s minimum gap.

Two valid routes to the Crownless King

Route A · Sever the bloodline

  • Kill the first add permanently.
  • Royal Lizard inherits Blizzard at 16s cadence.
  • Kill the second add permanently.
  • Royal Lizard inherits Storm at 24s cadence.

Safer boss HP state, but the Crown gains the full inherited kit.

Route B · Burn the Crown

  • Push Royal Lizard to 5% while adds live.
  • It consumes every survivor.
  • Each consumed add heals the boss by 5% maximum HP.
  • The Crown inherits each consumed bloodline’s power.

Fast initially, but creates extra boss HP and a dangerous solo endgame.

PHASE 4 · THE DROWNED CROWN06
Shared encounter systems

Positioning and the final barrage

Tidal Convergence · Phases 2–4

  1. Server checks live court positions every 5s.
  2. Two or more living players within 25 court units trigger a warning.
  3. The team has 3s to spread.
  4. If still grouped, the server recalculates each remaining cluster’s center.
  5. One coordinate-targeted Water Pillar rises at each cluster center.
  6. If they spread, the cast is cancelled and chat confirms success.

Last Tide barrage

  • Ten enemy-court SeaWaves.
  • Random X and random enemy-court depth.
  • One wave every second.
  • Native damage and tumble behavior.
  • No healing, resurrection, normal boss attacks, or rear waves.

Final Point cadence

  • Homing Ball every 6s
  • Water Pillar every 10s
  • Blizzard every 12s
  • Storm every 18s
  • Tidal Convergence returns

Victory: reduce Royal Lizard from 5% to zero.

The barrage is a survival gate, not the kill itself. Passing it earns one final, aggressive execution window against a vulnerable Crown.
SHARED SYSTEMS · LAST TIDE07
Designer reference

Readability, tuning, and playtest focus

Chat language hierarchy

  • Narrative state: ward, bloodline, Crown, Tide.
  • Action instruction: balance, spread, execute, endure.
  • Exact stakes: wave count, 10s window, health percentage.

Atmosphere frames the event; explicit language tells players what to do.

Encounter invariants

  • ✓ Exactly two Lizard-family adds
  • ✓ Boss immunity is tied to living wards only where stated
  • ✓ All SeaWaves travel from enemy court
  • ✓ No scripted Magma, Inferno, rear waves, or green zones
  • ✓ Support restrictions are announced
